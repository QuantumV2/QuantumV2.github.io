Ever since I was born, I was thinking about game design, straight up crawled out the womb and was like how can I make this experience more fun. This is true. Do not fact check this.

Let's discuss some rules I have derived since.


### 1. Power should be proportional to the difficulty to obtain it
<hr>
This sounds simple, real 2+2 shit right? Well NO. There's this minecraft mod called "Artifacts", which adds variout trinkets for you to use. The issue? They are almost in every fucking chest and their powers range from "You can eat grass to heal" to "You can jump tens of blocks into the air". They are found in cabins which are in no way difficult to spot, not difficult to get through, not difficult to loot, NOTHING. Literally just free shit. Annoying.

### 2. Any item that is decently powerful should have limited uses
<hr>
If you obtain a decently difficult to obtain item, then there is no way in hell you are ever going to do that again. First of all, because you already have it, second of all, because it was hard. This effectively cuts out a chunk of the gameplay, and if your game is on the shorter side that is awful. But remember, that it shouldnt be completely unfair just to pad out the game time.
